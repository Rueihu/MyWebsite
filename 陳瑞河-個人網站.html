01 自我介紹About

從系統維運現場，走向資料與 AIFrom the ops floor toward data & AI

我在資訊產業的第一線待了將近十年，從助理工程師、系統工程師到 IT 據點主管，習慣的是在使用者、系統與時間壓力之間，把問題一件一件排除、把環境穩定下來。2022 年起，我在南臺科技大學攻讀大數據分析專班碩士，用在職進修的方式重新打開另一個視角：如何從雜訊般的原始資料裡，整理出看得懂、用得上的洞察。碩士論文選擇分析公司實際的客訴資料，用 R 語言做文字探勘，這是我第一次完整跑過一次「從資料到結論」的過程。

I've spent close to a decade on the front lines of IT — from assistant engineer to systems engineer to on-site IT lead — getting comfortable working between users, systems, and time pressure to track down problems and keep environments stable. Starting in 2022, I pursued a part-time master's in Big Data Analytics at Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology, which opened up a different way of thinking: how to turn raw, noisy data into something legible and useful. For my thesis, I ran a text-mining analysis on a company's real customer complaint data using R — the first time I carried a project all the way from raw data to a conclusion.

我對自己的定位很清楚：IT 基礎建設仍是我最扎實、最有把握的核心能力；資料分析是我這幾年認真投入、還在持續累積案例與手感的第二專長；生成式 AI 工具則是我目前每天都在摸索、嘗試放進工作流程裡的新領域。比起把自己包裝成「數據分析師」，更貼切的說法是：一個對資料與 AI 保持好奇心、並且正在紮實學習的 IT 工程師。

I'm fairly clear-eyed about where I stand: IT infrastructure is still my strongest, most dependable skill set. Data analysis is a second discipline I've been seriously building over the past few years — still accumulating real cases and instincts. Generative AI tools are the newest area, one I experiment with almost daily and try to fold into how I work. Rather than packaging myself as a finished "data analyst," it's closer to the truth to say: an IT engineer who stays curious about data and AI, and is putting in the real work to learn them properly.
